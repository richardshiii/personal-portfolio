# Google BI Certificate Tableau Dashboard: Analyzing Minnesota Traffic Patterns for Transportation Planning
---
## Dashboard Overview
- This dashboard showcase a dynamic Tableau dashboard developed as part of the Google Business Intellifence Certificate course. The purpose of this dashboard is to transform traffic data into actionable insights for traffic planners and other key stakeholders. 
- Questions answered by this dashboard: 
- What are the overall traffic volume trends and how do they differ by time, holidays, and weather?
- Are there specific peaks or patterns that indicate potential areas for traffic management?
