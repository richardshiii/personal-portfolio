# Google BI Certificate Tableau Dashboard: Analyzing Minnesota Traffic Patterns for Transportation Planning
---
## Dashboard Overview
- This dashboard showcase a dynamic Tableau dashboard developed as part of the Google Business Intellifence Certificate course. The purpose of this dashboard is to transform traffic data into actionable insights for traffic planners and other key stakeholders. 
- Questions answered by this dashboard: 
    - What are the overall traffic volume trends and how do they differ by time, holidays, and weather?
    - Are there specific peaks or patterns that indicate potential areas for traffic management?
    - This dashboard showcases my ability to solve real-world problems, create interactive dashboards, and story-telling with data. 
---
##  Dataset Information
This dataset records hourly traffic volume data in relation to different weather conditions and holidays -- useful for analyzing traffic patterns and predicting traffic volume based on specific factors.

## Dashboard Highlights
1. Traffic volume analysis by month and year showing monthly and annual trends for comparisons, providing insights into seasonal variations. Users can also use datetime filter to compare specifc ranges of traffic flow. 
2. Impact of weather on traffic volume: highlights how various weather conditions impact traffic volume.
3. National holiday vs. traffic volume: designed a bubble chart to reflect the amount of traffic on certain national holidays, effectively showcase peak traffic volumes associated with public celebrations.
4. Traffic volumn breakdown dashboard: an interactive dashboard offering multi-faceted views of traffic volume impacted by various parameters, and showcases my proficiency in visualizing complex datasets for actionable insights.
